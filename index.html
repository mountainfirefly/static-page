01 · AI DECISION SYSTEMS

Smart Partner — Building a Trustworthy AI Decision Engine

Enterprise / Consulting · AI Engineering · RAG · Decision Systems · Agentic Workflows

The challenge

Enterprise expertise is often distributed across people, processes and internal knowledge. The goal was to turn that expertise into a repeatable AI-assisted diagnostic and recommendation workflow.

The solution

We designed a hybrid architecture where AI helps understand and communicate the problem, while the application controls diagnostic state, retrieval, ranking, confidence and stopping rules.

Business value

The pattern can be applied to consulting, support, sales qualification, compliance, internal knowledge and other workflows where expert knowledge needs to scale.

Your experts already know how to solve the problem. We can turn that expertise into an AI system that can scale.

Architecture

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Deterministic ranking
        ↓
AI explanation
        ↓
Recommendation

Engineering highlights

  • Dynamic discovery instead of a fixed questionnaire
  • Persistent diagnostic state
  • Information-gain based targeting
  • Structured knowledge corpus
  • Confidence gates and stopping rules
  • Deterministic fallback behaviour
